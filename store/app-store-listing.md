### Full description

```
Matsu's Blue Tears (the bioluminescent dinoflagellate Noctiluca scintillans) are a natural phenomenon. Whether you see them depends on season, moonlight, wind, sea temperature and tide. This app turns those conditions into a single "tonight index" and tells you the darkest, most promising window, so chasing the glow is no longer guesswork.

TONIGHT'S INDEX AND 7-DAY FORECAST
• 0–100 Blue Tears index with high / medium / low levels
• Best viewing window: the darkest stretch between sunset and moonrise
• Moon phase and illumination, high and low tide times, wind, rain probability
• Six factor bars that explain why tonight scores the way it does

VIEWING-SPOT MAP
• Ten spots across Nangan, Beigan, Juguang and Dongyin: Beihai Tunnel, Iron Fort, Dahan Stronghold, Jinsha, Qinbi, Qiaozai, Fuzheng, Dapu, Kunqiu and Dongyin
• Description, how to get there and tonight's suggested window for each spot
• Locate me: nearest spots with distance and one-tap directions
• Save your favourites

TRANSPORT AND ALERTS
• UNI AIR flights Taipei Songshan / Taichung ⇄ Nangan and Beigan
• Taima Star and New Taima ferries Keelung ⇄ Nangan ⇄ Dongyin
• Inter-island boats Nangan ⇄ Beigan, Juguang, Dongyin
• Live fog and strong-wind advisories from Taiwan's Central Weather Administration
• One tap to the official UNI AIR and Matsu ferry booking systems

STAY
• Meishi Officers' Tea House (historic building) and Da Meishi Co-living House
• Book directly on the official website

REMINDERS
• Get notified 30 minutes before tonight's best window begins

LANGUAGES
• Traditional Chinese / English, follows your system setting

DATA SOURCES
• Central Weather Administration Open Data Platform (township forecasts, tide forecasts, weather advisories, marine observations)
• Ministry of Transportation TDX platform (flights, ferries)
• Sunset, moonrise/moonset and moon phase computed with astronomical algorithms
• The Blue Tears index is this app's own estimate, not an official forecast. Nature offers no guarantees; conditions on site prevail.

Built by Meistone, who run a guesthouse and cultural space on Matsu and check this app every evening before heading out.
```

## 七、審核備註（App Review Notes，兩個商店共用）

```
This app is a native (Capacitor) shell around our own web client plus a serverless API we operate at https://matsu-blue-tears.vercel.app/api/v1. It is not a generic website wrapper: it adds device location for nearest-spot distance, local notifications for tonight's viewing window, offline caching of the last forecast, saved favourites, and bilingual UI.

Data come from Taiwan's Central Weather Administration open-data API and the TDX transport API. If those upstreams are unavailable the app clearly shows a "model estimate" badge and keeps working with astronomical calculations.

No account or login is required. Nothing in the app is behind a paywall. Booking buttons open our hotel's official website (https://matsu.live/book-a-room) in the system browser; no purchase happens inside the app.

Test tips: open "Forecast" and tap a day to expand factors; on "Map" tap "Locate me" (any location works, distances update); on "Me" turn on the reminder to see the notification permission flow.
```
